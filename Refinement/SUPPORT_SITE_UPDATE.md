## Support page addition

### Share contacts with Goldfish

Goldfish can share selected people in either a `.goldfish` file or a vCard. A Goldfish file can carry contact details, photos, saved locations, relationships between people in the selection, and pond memberships for selected people. Before sharing, you can choose whether to include notes.

When someone opens a Goldfish file, Goldfish shows a review with the people, relationship count, ponds, and note setting before import. Importing copies the shared information into that person’s app. It does not create an ongoing connection or synchronize later changes. Existing contact details and pond assignments are kept; missing details may be added. The recipient can cancel instead of importing.

A vCard is intended for compatible contacts apps. It may not preserve Goldfish relationships, pond membership, or other Goldfish-specific information. Share only information you are comfortable sending to the destination and recipient you choose.

### Contact and privacy questions

For help, contact **goldfish.pond.app@gmail.com**. Goldfish does not send a support request automatically from the app; you choose whether to use Mail, Share, or Copy.

## Privacy policy addition

Add this under the existing data sharing/export section, or use it to replace that subsection after reviewing the surrounding policy:

### Goldfish files and contact exports

Goldfish stores your journal in the app’s local database on your device. If you choose to create a `.goldfish` share, you select which people to include and whether to include notes. The file may contain those people’s contact details, photos, saved locations, relationships when both people are included, and their pond memberships. Goldfish displays a contents review before importing a received file. Import adds the shared information to the recipient’s local journal; it does not synchronize future changes. The recipient may keep existing contact details and pond assignments, while missing details may be added.

If you export or share a file, you choose the destination and recipient using the available sharing options. Goldfish does not send the file to its developer or operate a Goldfish sharing server. Once shared, the recipient and the service or app they use control their copy. A vCard is a contact export and may not preserve Goldfish relationships, pond memberships, or all location information. A Goldfish file is a transfer copy, not a backup or sync service.
